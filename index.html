❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply?
Click 'Apply Now' on any job card — you'll be redirected to WhatsApp with a pre-filled message mentioning the company. Our team will get back to you promptly with next steps.
Are opportunities verified?
Yes! Every job posting is manually reviewed and verified by the Eagle Career team for authenticity before it appears on the platform.
Can students apply?
Absolutely — we have dedicated roles for students, undergraduates, and fresh graduates. Use the Students filter to see all relevant listings.
Can foreigners apply?
Yes, many opportunities are specifically open to foreign nationals with English proficiency. Use the Foreigners filter to find these roles.
Do I need experience?
Not always! We regularly post entry-level, internship, and graduate roles that require zero prior experience. Check each listing's requirements for details.
